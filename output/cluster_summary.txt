Interpretation:
- Cluster 0 = personal conversation (4,550 msgs, 1.5% spam): just, ok, like, got, come, know, good, time...
- Cluster 1 = promotional/commercial (621 msgs, 93.9% spam): phonetoken, moneytoken, free, txt, urltoken, stop, claim, prize, cash...
- k=2 chosen: silhouette is low and flat for k=2..10 with no clear elbow; at every k one ~600-message cluster is 94-95% spam,
  and extra clusters only split ordinary chat into small topics. The dataset's main structure is promotional vs personal.
- Without using labels, 583/653 spam (89%) land in cluster 1; only 38 ham do. ARI=0.88, NMI=0.76, purity=0.98.
- Low silhouette (0.027) is expected for sparse high-dimensional text: clusters overlap near the boundary.
- Cluster IDs are arbitrary; only the composition is compared with the labels.
